Featured project

Medi-Pod

Self-Led Pilot Project · Queens, NY · Sep to Oct 2026

A 4-in-1 medicine dispenser for elderly users with memory loss.

An independent continuation of my Georgia Tech prototype with Allen Senior Home, revised around what users most reliably forget.

Tools
CAD, Design for Manufacturability (DFM), 3D Modeling & Prototyping, User Research
Results
Validated with 55 elders and caregivers. Working prototype demoed to Georgia Tech faculty and peers, showing its full mechanical function and user flow.
  • CompartmentsOne-push Morning, Noon, and Night
  • AlarmPitched low enough to clear hearing aids
  • DisplayLCD screen that shows only the date
  • MethodPrototyped in CAD with design-for-manufacturability (DFM) principles
  • Testing55 elders and caregivers at Allen Senior Home

How it developed

  1. Summer 2026 · Georgia TechListenRan direct user-research interviews with older adults and caregivers.
  2. Two-week sprintModelTurned their feedback into a fully 3D-modeled and animated CAD design.
  3. Summer 2026 · Georgia TechDemoPresented a live demo of the working prototype to Georgia Tech faculty and peers.
  4. Sep to Oct 2026 · Allen Senior HomeReviseRan interviews and feedback sessions with 55 elders and caregivers, then revised the design in CAD with DFM principles.
